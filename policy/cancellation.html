Policies & Terms

Please read our booking policies carefully before confirming any tour package with Wander & Wonder Travels.

Cancellation & Booking Policy

At Wander & Wonder Travels, we understand that travel plans may change unexpectedly. Our cancellation policy is designed to remain transparent and fair for all travellers.

Booking Confirmation

  • Bookings are confirmed only after receiving the required advance payment.
  • All bookings remain subject to hotel, transport and service availability.

Cancellation Charges

  • More than 30 days before departure: 20% cancellation charge
  • 15–30 days before departure: 50% cancellation charge
  • 7–14 days before departure: 75% cancellation charge
  • Less than 7 days before departure / No-show: No refund

Important Notes

  • Flight or train cancellation charges apply as per airline/railway rules.
  • Peak season, festivals and holidays may have different cancellation policies.
  • Approved refunds generally take 7–14 working days to process.

Refund Policy

Customer satisfaction is important to us. Refunds are processed according to the following conditions.

Refund Eligibility

  • Refunds apply only for eligible cancellations covered under our cancellation policy.
  • Unused services during the trip remain non-refundable.

Refund Process

  • Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method whenever possible.
  • Processing time generally takes 7–14 business days depending upon banks/payment gateways.

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Last minute cancellations
  • No-shows
  • Personal emergencies during travel
  • Weather issues, natural disasters, strikes or government restrictions
  • Transport delays beyond our control

Tour Changes

In rare situations itineraries, hotels or transportation may change due to operational reasons. Equivalent alternatives will be arranged whenever possible.

Booking Terms

  • Full payment must be completed before tour commencement unless otherwise agreed.

Travel Documents

  • Loss of travel documents remains the traveller’s responsibility.

Traveller Responsibilities

  • Proper behaviour is expected throughout the journey.

Liability

Tour schedules may change due to unforeseen circumstances.

Health & Safety

  • Travellers should ensure they are medically fit before joining the tour.
  • Adventure activities are undertaken entirely at personal risk.

Pricing

Tour prices may change due to fuel costs, supplier revisions, taxes or government regulations before final confirmation.

Acceptance

By booking with Wander & Wonder Travels, you acknowledge that you have read and accepted all policies, terms and conditions.

Privacy Policy

Wander & Wonder Travels respects your privacy and protects personal information shared through enquiries, bookings and website usage.

  • Personal details collected may include name, phone number, email and travel preferences.
  • Information is used only for bookings, communication and service improvement.
  • We do not sell or share customer information with unrelated third parties.
  • Payment information is handled through secure payment providers.
  • Users may contact us to update or remove personal information when applicable.
