Adventure Activities in Andaman Islands: Complete Guide to Scuba Diving, Snorkeling, Sea Walking, Parasailing & More (2026)

The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are not only famous for their pristine beaches and crystal-clear waters but are also India's ultimate adventure tourism destination. Surrounded by vibrant coral reefs, turquoise lagoons, tropical forests, and rich marine biodiversity, the islands offer some of the most exciting water and nature-based adventure activities in Asia.

Whether you are a beginner seeking your first underwater experience or an experienced adventure enthusiast looking for world-class diving sites, Andaman has something for everyone.

From scuba diving among colorful coral reefs to sea walking on the ocean floor, kayaking through mangrove forests, parasailing over the Bay of Bengal, and game fishing in deep waters, the possibilities are endless.

This guide covers every major adventure activity available in Andaman, including the best locations, age requirements, approximate costs, difficulty levels, safety considerations, and ideal seasons.

Why Andaman is India's Best Adventure Destination

Several factors make Andaman unique among Indian adventure destinations.

Crystal Clear Water

Visibility often exceeds 20 to 30 meters, making underwater activities exceptionally enjoyable.

Rich Coral Reefs

The islands host some of India's healthiest coral reef ecosystems.

Diverse Marine Life

Visitors frequently encounter a wide variety of marine species during water activities.

Marine Creatures Commonly Seen

  • Clownfish
  • Parrotfish
  • Sea Turtles
  • Stingrays
  • Reef Sharks
  • Barracudas
  • Moray Eels
  • Octopus

Suitable for Beginners

Most adventure activities in Andaman are beginner-friendly and require little or no previous experience.

International Standards

Many scuba diving centers operate under internationally recognized organizations such as PADI and SSI, ensuring high safety standards and professional training.

1. Scuba Diving in Andaman

Scuba diving is undoubtedly the most popular adventure activity in Andaman. The islands are often considered the scuba diving capital of India.

Unlike snorkeling, scuba diving allows participants to descend several meters below the surface and experience the underwater world up close.

The vibrant coral reefs, tropical fish, sea turtles, and crystal-clear visibility make Andaman one of the best diving destinations in Asia.

Best Places for Scuba Diving

Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)

Popular dive sites include:

  • Nemo Reef
  • Aquarium
  • Lighthouse
  • Seduction Point
  • Turtle Bay
  • Johnny's Gorge

Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep)

  • Healthy coral reefs
  • Excellent underwater visibility
  • Less crowded dive sites
  • Ideal for beginner and intermediate divers

North Bay Island

North Bay is one of the most popular locations for introductory scuba diving and first-time divers.

Cinque Island

Offers some of the most untouched coral reefs and pristine underwater environments in the Andaman Islands.

Types of Scuba Diving

Discover Scuba Diving

Designed specifically for beginners who want to experience scuba diving for the first time.

  • No swimming knowledge required
  • Professional instructor accompanies participants
  • Maximum depth: 6–12 meters
  • Underwater duration: 30–45 minutes
  • Ideal for first-time visitors

Fun Diving

Suitable for certified divers looking to explore Andaman's famous dive sites.

  • Requires scuba certification
  • Depth: 18–30 meters
  • Multiple dive site options
  • Suitable for experienced divers

Advanced Diving

Perfect for adventure enthusiasts seeking specialized underwater experiences.

  • Deep Diving
  • Night Diving
  • Wreck Diving
  • Drift Diving

Scuba Diving Cost

  • Discover Scuba: ₹3,500 – ₹6,500
  • Shore Dive: ₹4,000 – ₹7,000
  • Boat Dive: ₹5,000 – ₹10,000
  • PADI Open Water Certification: ₹20,000 – ₹35,000

Minimum Age

10 years and above.

Best Time for Scuba Diving

October to May offers the best visibility, calm sea conditions, and ideal weather for scuba diving.

2. Snorkeling in Andaman

Snorkeling is perfect for travelers who wish to explore marine life without diving deep underwater.

Participants float on the water surface while observing colorful coral reefs and tropical fish below.

Best Snorkeling Locations

  • Elephant Beach – Most famous snorkeling destination in Andaman.
  • North Bay Island – Excellent coral formations.
  • Jolly Buoy Island – Crystal-clear visibility.
  • Red Skin Island – Rich marine biodiversity.

What You Can See

  • Coral Reefs
  • Tropical Fish
  • Sea Cucumbers
  • Giant Clams
  • Sea Turtles

Suitable Age

5 years and above.

Approximate Cost

₹500 – ₹2,500 per person.

3. Sea Walking

Sea walking is one of the most unique experiences available in Andaman.

Participants wear a specially designed oxygen-supplied helmet and walk comfortably on the seabed while interacting with marine life.

Best Locations

  • North Bay Island
  • Elephant Beach

Suitable Age

10–55 years.

Cost

₹3,000 – ₹5,500 per person.

Duration

20–30 minutes underwater.

4. Parasailing

Parasailing combines boating and flying, offering stunning aerial views of Andaman's coastline and turquoise waters.

Best Locations

  • Corbyn's Cove Beach
  • North Bay Island
  • Rajiv Gandhi Water Sports Complex

Age Requirement

12 years and above.

Cost

₹2,500 – ₹4,500 per person.

5. Jet Skiing

Jet skiing is among the most thrilling high-speed water sports available in Andaman.

Best Locations

  • Corbyn's Cove Beach
  • North Bay Island
  • Elephant Beach

Cost

₹600 – ₹1,500

Age Requirement

12 years and above.

6. Banana Boat Ride

Perfect for groups and families, banana boat rides offer fun, excitement, and laughter as the inflatable boat speeds across the sea.

Cost

₹500 – ₹1,000

Suitable Age

6 years and above.

7. Glass Bottom Boat Ride

Ideal for non-swimmers, children, and elderly travelers who wish to enjoy marine life without entering the water.

Best Locations

  • North Bay Island
  • Jolly Buoy Island
  • Elephant Beach

Cost

₹500 – ₹1,500

Safety Tips for Adventure Activities in Andaman

While adventure activities in Andaman are generally safe and professionally managed, following basic safety precautions can significantly enhance your experience and ensure a hassle-free trip.

Important Safety Guidelines

  • Always choose licensed and certified activity operators.
  • Follow instructor briefings and safety instructions carefully.
  • Wear life jackets and safety equipment whenever required.
  • Avoid water activities during rough weather or high tides.
  • Inform instructors about any medical conditions, asthma, heart issues, or recent surgeries.
  • Stay hydrated throughout the day, especially during summer.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen to protect marine ecosystems.
  • Avoid touching corals or disturbing marine life.
  • Do not consume alcohol before participating in water sports.
  • Keep valuables in waterproof bags while engaging in activities.

Conclusion

The Andaman Islands offer one of the most diverse collections of adventure activities anywhere in India. From world-class scuba diving and snorkeling experiences to thrilling parasailing, jet skiing, sea walking, deep-sea fishing, and kayaking through serene mangrove forests, the islands provide something for every type of traveler.

Whether you are visiting with family, planning a honeymoon, traveling solo, or seeking an adrenaline-filled vacation with friends, Andaman's blend of natural beauty and adventure opportunities creates memories that last a lifetime.

The crystal-clear waters, healthy coral reefs, abundant marine biodiversity, and professionally managed adventure infrastructure make Andaman the undisputed adventure tourism capital of India.

A well-planned Andaman holiday combines pristine beaches, unforgettable underwater experiences, tropical island charm, and exciting outdoor adventures that few destinations in the country can match.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Which is the best adventure activity in Andaman?

Scuba diving is considered the most popular and rewarding adventure activity in Andaman due to its world-class coral reefs and marine biodiversity.

Can non-swimmers enjoy water activities in Andaman?

Yes. Non-swimmers can participate in sea walking, introductory scuba diving, parasailing, glass-bottom boat rides, and several other activities.

What is the best time for adventure activities in Andaman?

October to May offers the best weather, calm sea conditions, and excellent underwater visibility.

How much does scuba diving cost in Andaman?

Scuba diving generally costs between ₹3,500 and ₹10,000 depending on the dive site and package selected.

Which island is best for scuba diving?

Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) is widely regarded as the best destination for scuba diving in Andaman.

Is Andaman suitable for family adventure trips?

Yes. Activities such as snorkeling, glass-bottom boat rides, banana boat rides, kayaking, and beach exploration make Andaman ideal for families.
