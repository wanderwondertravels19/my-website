Kashmir in Winter 2026: Complete Snowfall Guide

For travelers dreaming of snow-covered mountains, frozen lakes, skiing adventures, and magical winter landscapes, Kashmir transforms into a true winter wonderland between December and February. Winter is the best season to witness snowfall in Kashmir, particularly in Gulmarg, Sonamarg, and higher-altitude regions.

From snow-covered pine forests to skiing on world-class slopes, winter offers a completely different experience compared to the green summer valleys.

When Does Snowfall Start in Kashmir?

Snowfall generally begins in higher-altitude regions by late November and becomes widespread from December onwards.

Typical Snowfall Season in Kashmir

Month Snowfall Chances
November Light snowfall in Gulmarg and Sonamarg
December Snowfall begins regularly
January Peak snowfall season
February Heavy snowfall continues
March Snow remains in Gulmarg, occasional snowfall
April Snow visible at higher altitudes

Best Time for Guaranteed Snowfall

If your primary goal is to experience snowfall, plan your trip between January 5 and February 20.

During this period, snowfall is most frequent and snow accumulation is usually at its highest.

Which Places Receive the Most Snowfall?

Gulmarg

Altitude: Approximately 2,650 meters

Gulmarg receives the heaviest tourist-friendly snowfall in Kashmir and is India's premier ski destination.

Winter Highlights:

  • Thick snow cover
  • Skiing and snowboarding
  • Gondola rides
  • Snowmobile adventures
  • Snow trekking

Average Winter Snow Depth:

  • December: 1–3 feet
  • January: 3–8 feet
  • February: 4–10 feet

Sonamarg

Sonamarg experiences heavy snowfall and often becomes inaccessible during severe winter weather.

Winter Highlights:

  • Frozen landscapes
  • Snow-covered mountains
  • Photography opportunities

Road conditions may become unpredictable during heavy snowfall.

Pahalgam

Pahalgam receives moderate snowfall and offers beautiful winter scenery.

Best Winter Attractions:

  • Betaab Valley
  • Aru Valley
  • Lidder River

Ideal for travelers who want snow without extreme weather conditions.

Srinagar

Srinagar receives occasional snowfall during winter.

Highlights:

  • Snow-covered Dal Lake surroundings
  • Frozen garden landscapes
  • Winter houseboat experience

Although snowfall is not guaranteed every day, Srinagar becomes incredibly beautiful after fresh snow.

Winter Clothing Checklist for Kashmir

Many first-time visitors underestimate how cold Kashmir can become during winter. Proper winter clothing is essential for a comfortable and enjoyable trip.

Essential Clothing

Thermal Wear

Carry:

  • 2–3 Thermal Tops
  • 2–3 Thermal Bottoms

Thermals provide the base layer of warmth and help maintain body temperature during freezing conditions.

Winter Jacket

Choose:

  • Waterproof Jacket
  • Down Jacket
  • Insulated Winter Coat

A good quality winter jacket is the most important investment for a Kashmir winter trip.

Woolen Sweaters

Carry at least:

  • 2 Woolen Sweaters
  • 1 Fleece Jacket

Layering clothing helps regulate body temperature and provides additional warmth when needed.

Gloves

Recommended:

  • Waterproof Gloves
  • Thermal Gloves

Hands often become cold first during snowfall, making gloves an essential winter accessory.

Woolen Cap

A woolen cap or beanie helps retain body heat and protects your ears from freezing winds.

Muffler or Neck Warmer

A neck warmer protects against icy winds, especially in Gulmarg, Sonamarg, and other high-altitude destinations.

Woolen Socks

Carry:

  • 4–5 Pairs of Woolen Socks

Wet feet become uncomfortable very quickly in snowy conditions, so extra socks are always useful.

Snow Boots

Essential For:

  • Gulmarg
  • Sonamarg
  • Heavy Snowfall Periods

Waterproof snow boots prevent snow and slush from entering your footwear and provide better grip on slippery surfaces.

Winter Travel Accessories

Recommended Accessories:

  • Sunglasses
  • Lip Balm
  • Moisturizer
  • Sunscreen
  • Power Bank
  • Hand Warmers

Even during winter, UV exposure at high altitudes can be surprisingly strong. Sunglasses and sunscreen are highly recommended.

Travel Tip: Waterproof footwear and thermal clothing can make a significant difference to your comfort level during a winter trip to Kashmir.

Important Winter Travel Tips

  • Book hotels and flights well in advance during peak winter season.
  • Keep at least one buffer day in your itinerary for weather-related delays.
  • Monitor weather forecasts regularly before and during your trip.
  • Carry sufficient cash when visiting remote areas.
  • Check Gulmarg Gondola operational status during heavy snowfall.
  • Follow local advisories regarding road closures and weather warnings.
  • Avoid driving on snow-covered roads without prior experience.
  • Keep emergency contact numbers readily available.

Frequently Asked Questions About Kashmir Snowfall & Winter Travel

When does snowfall start in Kashmir?

Snowfall typically begins in higher-altitude areas such as Gulmarg and Sonamarg during late November or early December. Regular snowfall usually occurs between December and February.

Which month receives the heaviest snowfall in Kashmir?

January is generally the month with the heaviest snowfall in Kashmir. Gulmarg often experiences several feet of accumulated snow during this period.

What is the best month to see snow in Kashmir?

January and February are considered the best months for guaranteed snowfall and snow-related activities.

Can I see snowfall in Kashmir in December?

Yes. Snowfall often begins in December, especially in Gulmarg, Sonamarg, and other higher-altitude regions. However, snowfall intensity may vary from year to year.

Is there snow in Gulmarg during March?

Yes. Gulmarg usually has significant snow cover throughout March, making it a good choice for travelers who want snow with slightly warmer temperatures.

Does Srinagar receive snowfall?

Yes. Srinagar receives occasional snowfall during winter, usually between December and February. While snowfall is less frequent than in Gulmarg, fresh snow transforms the city into a beautiful winter destination.

Is Gulmarg better than Pahalgam for snowfall?

Gulmarg receives significantly more snowfall and is the best destination for skiing and winter sports. Pahalgam offers beautiful winter scenery but generally receives less snow.

How cold does Kashmir get in winter?

Temperatures vary by location. Srinagar may experience temperatures between -2°C and 10°C, while Gulmarg can drop to -10°C or lower during severe winter conditions.

Is January too cold for visiting Kashmir?

January is cold but remains the most popular month for travelers seeking snowfall. Proper winter clothing makes sightseeing and snow activities comfortable.

What clothes should I carry for Kashmir in winter?

Essential winter items include:

  • Thermal wear
  • Heavy winter jacket
  • Woolen sweaters
  • Gloves
  • Woolen cap
  • Woolen socks
  • Waterproof snow boots
  • Neck warmer

Frequently Asked Questions

Do hotels provide room heaters in Kashmir?

Most hotels provide heating facilities during winter. Luxury hotels generally offer central heating, while budget hotels may provide electric heaters upon request.

Are snow boots necessary in Gulmarg?

Yes. Waterproof snow boots are strongly recommended during winter, especially from December to March when snow accumulation is significant.

Is the Gulmarg Gondola operational during snowfall?

The Gondola generally operates during winter, but services may be temporarily suspended during heavy snowfall, strong winds, or adverse weather conditions.

Is Kashmir safe to visit during winter?

Yes. Major tourist destinations such as Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam remain popular winter destinations. Travelers should monitor weather conditions and follow local advisories.

Can families travel to Kashmir during snowfall?

Absolutely. Kashmir is a popular family destination during winter. Children particularly enjoy snow activities such as sledging, snowball fights, and snowmobile rides.

How many days are enough for a Kashmir snowfall trip?

A 5 to 7-day itinerary is ideal for covering Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam while enjoying snowfall and winter activities.

What is the average cost of a Kashmir snowfall trip?

A winter trip typically costs:

  • Budget: ₹15,000–₹25,000 per person
  • Mid-range: ₹25,000–₹45,000 per person
  • Luxury: ₹50,000–₹1,20,000+ per person

Actual expenses depend on flight prices, hotel category, and activities chosen.

Which destination receives the most snowfall in Kashmir?

Gulmarg receives the heaviest and most reliable snowfall among Kashmir's major tourist destinations and is widely regarded as India's premier snow destination.

Can I visit Sonamarg in winter?

Access to Sonamarg may be restricted during periods of heavy snowfall. Travelers should check current road conditions before planning a winter visit.

Is February a good time to visit Kashmir?

Yes. February offers excellent snowfall, fewer crowds than the New Year season, and ideal conditions for skiing, snowboarding, and winter sightseeing.

If your dream is to witness snowfall, enjoy skiing, ride the Gulmarg Gondola through snowy landscapes, and experience Kashmir's magical winter charm, then January and February are undoubtedly the best months to visit Kashmir.

These months offer the highest chances of fresh snowfall, deep snow cover, and ideal conditions for winter sports and snow-based activities.

However, travelers seeking lush green valleys, blooming gardens, and comfortable sightseeing weather may prefer visiting during spring or summer.

Winter Kashmir offers a completely different experience—one filled with snow-covered valleys, frozen landscapes, peaceful surroundings, and unforgettable Himalayan scenery.
