Shimla Tourism Guide – The Queen of Hills in Himachal Pradesh

Nestled amidst the majestic Himalayas, Shimla is one of India’s most loved hill stations. Famous for colonial charm, pine-covered mountains, toy train rides, snowfall, bustling Mall Road and pleasant weather, Shimla attracts travellers throughout the year. Whether planning a honeymoon, family vacation or peaceful mountain escape, Shimla offers the perfect blend of nature, history and adventure.

Why Shimla is Famous Among Tourists
  • Pleasant weather throughout the year
  • Snowfall during winter
  • Heritage toy train journey
  • Adventure sports nearby
  • Colonial architecture
  • Easy accessibility from Delhi & Chandigarh

History of Shimla

The history of Shimla dates back to the British colonial era. Before British settlement, the region was inhabited by local hill tribes and covered with dense forests. After the Anglo-Gurkha War in 1815, the British took control of the region. By 1864, Shimla officially became the Summer Capital of British India. Government officials moved here during summers leading to construction of churches, schools and railway lines.

Many iconic landmarks still remain:

  • Christ Church
  • Viceregal Lodge
  • Gaiety Theatre
  • Shimla Railway Station

Best Places to Visit in Shimla

1. The Ridge

The Ridge is the heart of Shimla and among the most famous open spaces in town. Surrounded by colonial buildings and mountain views, it is perfect for photography and evening walks.

2. Mall Road

Mall Road is Shimla’s busiest tourist area filled with cafés, bakeries, handicraft shops and restaurants. Popular attractions nearby:

  • Scandal Point
  • Christ Church
  • Gaiety Theatre

3. Christ Church

Christ Church is among North India’s oldest churches. Its neo-Gothic architecture and glowing evening lights make it one of Shimla’s most photographed landmarks.

4. Kufri

Located around 16 km from Shimla, Kufri is famous for snow activities, horse riding and Himalayan views. Popular activities:

  • Skiing
  • ATV rides
  • Snowboarding
  • Horse riding

5. Jakhoo Temple

Dedicated to Lord Hanuman, Jakhoo Temple sits atop the highest point in Shimla and offers panoramic mountain views.

6. Kalka–Shimla Toy Train

The UNESCO-listed Kalka–Shimla Railway remains one of India’s greatest engineering marvels.

Best Time to Visit Shimla

Summer (March–June)

Pleasant weather ideal for sightseeing and honeymoon trips. Temperature: 15°C–30°C

Monsoon (July–September)

Lush green landscapes with fewer crowds.

Winter (October–February)

Snowfall experience, snow activities and romantic atmosphere. December–January are ideal for snow lovers.

Things to Do in Shimla

  • Explore colonial architecture
  • Shop local handicrafts

How to Reach Shimla

By Road

Connected from Delhi and Chandigarh.

By Train

Nearest broad-gauge station is Kalka.

By Air

Nearest airport: Jubbarhatti Airport.
