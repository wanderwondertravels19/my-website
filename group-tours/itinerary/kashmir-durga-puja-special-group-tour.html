Tour Itinerary

Day 1ˢᵗ: Journey Begins from Kolkata Railway Station

Meet and board the train from Kolkata Railway Station as your exciting journey towards Jammu & Kashmir begins. Overnight stay in train.

Day 2ⁿᵈ: Full Day Train Journey

Enjoy the scenic train journey throughout the day while travelling towards Jammu. Overnight stay in train.

Day 3ʳᵈ: Arrival at Jammu & Transfer to Srinagar | Dal Lake Shikara Ride

Arrive at Jammu Railway Station and proceed towards Srinagar by road, enjoying the scenic landscapes and beautiful mountain views along the way. Upon reaching Srinagar in the evening, experience a memorable Shikara ride on Dal Lake, surrounded by floating gardens and stunning Himalayan scenery. Overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 4ᵗʰ: Excursion to Gulmarg

After breakfast, proceed for a full-day excursion to Gulmarg, known for its breathtaking meadows, snow-covered landscapes, and adventure activities. Explore the scenic beauty and enjoy leisure time amidst the Himalayas. Return to Srinagar for an overnight stay.

Day 5ᵗʰ: Excursion to Sonamarg

Day 5ᵗʰ: Excursion to Sonamarg After breakfast, proceed for a full-day excursion to Sonamarg, popularly known as the “Meadow of Gold.” Enjoy the breathtaking mountain landscapes, flowing rivers, and picturesque valleys surrounded by snow-capped peaks. Spend leisure time amidst the serene beauty before returning to Srinagar. Overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 6ᵗʰ: Srinagar Local Sightseeing & Shopping

Explore the major attractions of Srinagar including the famous Mughal Gardens, Shankaracharya Temple, and Lal Chowk. Enjoy local shopping for Kashmiri handicrafts, dry fruits, and traditional souvenirs. Overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 7ᵗʰ: Srinagar to Pahalgam via Scenic Route | Mini Switzerland & Cultural Evening.

After breakfast, travel towards Pahalgam while visiting saffron fields, apple orchards, and a traditional cricket bat manufacturing factory en route. Reach Pahalgam and enjoy an evening visit to the picturesque Mini Switzerland (Baisaran Valley). At night, enjoy a bonfire session with music and entertainment. Overnight stay in Pahalgam.

Day 8ᵗʰ: Aru Valley & Betaab Valley Sightseeing | Transfer to Jammu

Visit the beautiful Aru Valley and Betaab Valley, renowned for stunning landscapes and river views. Later, begin the journey towards Jammu Railway Station and board the train for your return journey. Overnight stay in train.

Day 9ᵗʰ: Full Day Train Journey

Spend the day onboard the train while travelling back, carrying unforgettable memories of Kashmir. Overnight stay in train.

Day 10ᵗʰ: Arrival at Kolkata

Arrive at Kolkata Railway Station with wonderful memories of the trip, marking the end of the tour.
