Tour Itinerary

Day 01: Departure from Howrah – Overnight Train Journey

Your memorable journey to the "Abode of Clouds" begins from Howrah Railway Station. Board the scheduled overnight train to Guwahati and settle in for a comfortable journey as you travel towards the enchanting landscapes of Northeast India.

Day 02: Arrival at Guwahati – Umananda Temple & Brahmaputra River Cruise

Upon arrival at Guwahati Railway Station, our representative will assist with your hotel transfer. After check-in, visit the sacred Umananda Temple on Peacock Island. In the evening, enjoy a scenic Brahmaputra River Cruise and witness a beautiful sunset over the mighty river.

Day 03: Kamakhya Temple – Shillong

Begin the day with blessings at the sacred Kamakhya Temple, then drive to Shillong. En route, visit the breathtaking Laitlum Canyon and Shillong Peak for panoramic views. After hotel check-in, spend the evening exploring the lively Police Bazar. Sightseeing: Kamakhya Temple Laitlum Canyon Shillong Peak Police Bazar Overnight Stay: Shillong

Day 4: Hidden Gems of Shillong

After breakfast, explore the scenic beauty of Shillong. Visit Mawphlang Valley, enjoy the picturesque Bulls Trek, and explore the ancient Mawphlang Sacred Forest. Later, visit Elephant Falls and the Indian Air Force Museum. Sightseeing: Markham Valley (Dzukou of Meghalaya) Mawphlang Sacred Forest Elephant Falls Indian Air Force Museum Overnight Stay: Shillong

Day 05: Shillong – Cherrapunji

After breakfast, drive to Cherrapunji (Sohra). En route, stop at the scenic Mawkdok Dympep Valley Viewpoint. Continue exploring Arwah Cave, Nohkalikai Falls, Seven Sisters Falls, Mawsmai Cave, Bangladesh Plains View Point, and Kynrem Falls before checking in to your hotel. Sightseeing: Mawkdok Dympep Valley Viewpoint Arwah Cave Nohkalikai Falls Seven Sisters Falls Mawsmai Cave Bangladesh Plains View Point Kynrem Falls Overnight Stay: Cherrapunji

Day 06: Cherrapunji – Mawlynnong – Dawki

After breakfast, visit Mawlynnong, the "Cleanest Village in Asia," and explore its charming lanes and the famous Riwai Living Root Bridge. En route, admire the beauty of Rymbem Falls and Borhill Falls before continuing to Dawki. Check in to your riverside stay and relax for the evening. Sightseeing: Mawlynnong Village Riwai Living Root Bridge Rymbem Falls Borhill Falls Overnight Stay: Dawki

Day 07: Dawki – Krang Suri Falls – Guwahati

Start your day with the iconic Dawki Boating on the crystal-clear Umngot River. After breakfast, visit the breathtaking Krang Suri Falls, then begin your scenic drive back to Guwahati. Upon arrival, board your overnight train to Kolkata. Sightseeing: Dawki Boating Umngot River Krang Suri Falls Return Drive to Guwahati Board Overnight Train Overnight: Train

Day 08: Arrival at Kolkata

Arrive at Howrah/Kolkata Railway Station in the morning with unforgettable memories of Meghalaya's waterfalls, caves, pristine rivers, scenic valleys, and vibrant culture. Your journey concludes with cherished experiences and countless beautiful moments from the "Scotland of the East."
