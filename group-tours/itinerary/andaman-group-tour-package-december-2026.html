Tour Itinerary

Day 01 – Port Blair Arrival

Upon arrival at Port Blair Airport, our representative will receive you and assist with your hotel transfer. After check-in and some relaxation, begin your Andaman journey with local sightseeing.

  • Corbyn's Cove Beach
  • Cellular Jail
  • Light & Sound Show at Cellular Jail
  • Overnight Stay at Port Blair

Day 02 - Port Blair to Havelock Island

After breakfast, proceed to the jetty and board a ferry to Havelock Island. Upon arrival, check in to your hotel and later visit the world-famous Radhanagar Beach.

  • Morning Ferry Transfer to Havelock Island
  • Hotel Check-in
  • Radhanagar Beach
  • Sunset View
  • Overnight Stay at Havelock Island

Day 03: Havelock Island Excursion

Enjoy a day of leisure or choose from exciting water activities.

  • Optional Visit to Elephant Beach
  • Speed Boat Transfer
  • Snorkeling & Water Activities (Optional)
  • Leisure Time at Beaches and Cafés
  • Overnight Stay at Havelock Island

Day 04: Havelock Island to Neil Island

After breakfast, board a ferry to Neil Island and explore its beautiful beaches and natural attractions.

  • Ferry Transfer to Neil Island
  • Bharatpur Beach
  • Natural Bridge (Howrah Bridge)
  • Laxmanpur Beach Sunset Point
  • Overnight Stay at Neil Island

Day 05: Neil Island to Port Blair via Ross Island & North Bay Island

Return to Port Blair and enjoy an excursion to two of the most popular islands near the capital.

  • Ferry Transfer to Port Blair
  • Ross Island (Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Island)
  • North Bay Island
  • Overnight Stay at Port Blair

Day 06: Baratang Island Excursion

Embark on an early morning excursion to Baratang Island through the dense tropical forests of the Andaman Trunk Road.

  • Mangrove Boat Ride
  • Limestone Caves
  • Mud Volcano
  • Return to Port Blair
  • Overnight Stay at Port Blair

Day 07: Departure from Port Blair

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and enjoy some free time for shopping before your airport transfer.

  • Shopping at Aberdeen Bazaar
  • Airport Drop
  • Tour Ends with Wonderful Memories of Andaman
