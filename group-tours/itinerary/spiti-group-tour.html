Tour Itinerary

Day 1ˢᵗ: Journey Begins from Howrah Railway Station

Meet and Board Netaji Express from Howrah Railway Station at 9:55 PM, marking the beginning of your exciting Himachal journey. Overnight stay in train.

Day 2ⁿᵈ: Full Day Train Journey

Enjoy the train journey throughout the day while travelling towards Chandigarh. Overnight stay in train.

Day 3ʳᵈ: Arrival at Chandigarh | Transfer to Narkanda (173 km)

Pickup from Chandigarh and proceed towards Narkanda, enjoying scenic mountain roads and Himalayan landscapes along the journey. Overnight stay in Narkanda.

Day 4ᵗʰ: Narkanda to Sangla (189 km)

Begin the journey towards Sangla while passing through beautiful Himalayan regions including Kufri and the Gateway of Kinnaur. Reach Sangla and enjoy the serene valley atmosphere. Overnight stay in Sangla.

Day 5ᵗʰ: Sangla – Chitkul – Kalpa (72 km)

Sangla to Kalpa Stay.Explore Rakcham Village, Chitkul, Baspa River side, and Kinnaur Kailash View Point before proceeding towards Kalpa. Overnight stay in Kalpa.

Day 6ᵗʰ: Kalpa to Tabo (160 km)

Travel towards Tabo while visiting Khab Sangam Point, Nako Monastery, Nako Lake, Gue Monastery, and scenic Himalayan landscapes en route. Overnight stay in Tabo.

Day 7ᵗʰ: Tabo to Kaza via Pin Valley (98 km)

Proceed towards Kaza through Pin Valley while exploring Dhankar Monastery, Mud Village, and Kungri Monastery. Overnight stay in Kaza.

Day 8ᵗʰ: Kaza Local Sightseeing (Approx. 100 km)

Explore the World's Highest Post Office, Komik Monastery, Langza Buddha Statue, Key Monastery, Kibber Village, and Chicham Bridge. Overnight stay in Kaza.

Day 9ᵗʰ: Kaza to Chandratal (111 km)

Travel towards Chandratal crossing the scenic Kunzum Pass. Enjoy the breathtaking beauty of Chandratal Lake and experience a memorable night under the star-filled Himalayan sky. Overnight stay at Chandratal campsite.

Day 10ᵗʰ: Chandratal to Manali (110 km)

Proceed towards Manali while visiting Atal Tunnel, Solang Valley, and Sissu. Reach Manali and relax after the adventurous journey. Overnight stay in Manali.

Day 11ᵗʰ: Manali to Kasol (80 km)

After breakfast, depart from Manali and proceed towards Kasol, known for its scenic landscapes, Parvati River, and peaceful atmosphere. Spend leisure time exploring Kasol and nearby surroundings. Overnight stay in Kasol.

Day 12ᵗʰ: Kasol to Chandigarh Railway Station Drop (240 km)

Begin the return journey from Kasol to Chandigarh Railway Station, marking the end of an unforgettable Spiti Valley adventure filled with mountains, monasteries, lakes, and Himalayan experiences.

Day 13ᵗʰ: Full Day Train Journey

Spend the day onboard the train while travelling back, carrying unforgettable memories of Himachal Pradesh. Overnight stay in train.

Day 14ᵗʰ: Arrival at Kolkata

Arrive at Kolkata in the morning, marking the end of a memorable Himachal tour filled with mountains, valleys, adventure, and scenic experiences.
