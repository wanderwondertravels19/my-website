Tour Itinerary

Day 1ˢᵗ: Journey Begins from Kolkata Railway Station

Meet and board the train from Kolkata Railway Station as your exciting journey towards Jammu & Kashmir begins. Overnight stay in train.

Day 2ⁿᵈ: Full Day Train Journey

Enjoy the scenic train journey throughout the day while travelling towards Jammu. Overnight stay in train.

Day 3ʳᵈ: Arrival at Jammu | Transfer to Pahalgam

Arrive at Jammu Railway Station and proceed towards Pahalgam by road, enjoying scenic mountain landscapes and beautiful valleys along the way. Upon arrival, relax and spend the evening amidst the peaceful surroundings of Pahalgam. Overnight stay in Pahalgam.

Day 4ᵗʰ: Pahalgam Sightseeing | Transfer to Srinagar & Evening Shikara Ride

After breakfast, explore the beautiful attractions of Pahalgam including Mini Switzerland (Baisaran Valley), Aru Valley, and Betaab Valley, known for their breathtaking landscapes and serene surroundings. Later, proceed towards Srinagar while visiting the famous saffron fields, apple orchards, and traditional cricket bat manufacturing factory en route. Upon reaching Srinagar in the evening, enjoy a memorable Shikara ride on Dal Lake, surrounded by floating gardens and stunning Himalayan scenery. Overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 5ᵗʰ: Excursion to Gulmarg

Proceed for a full-day excursion to Gulmarg, known for its breathtaking meadows, snow-covered landscapes, and adventure activities. Explore the scenic beauty and enjoy leisure time amidst the Himalayas before returning to Srinagar. Overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 6ᵗʰ: Doodhpathri Excursion & Srinagar Local Sightseeing

In the first half of the day, visit Doodhpathri, famous for its lush green meadows, flowing streams, and serene surroundings. Later, return to Srinagar and explore popular attractions including Mughal Gardens, Shankaracharya Temple, and Lal Chowk. Overnight stay in Srinagar

Day 7ᵗʰ: Srinagar Shopping | Vande Bharat to Jammu | Board Return Train

Spend the morning exploring local markets and shopping in Srinagar. Enjoy local shopping for Kashmiri handicrafts, dry fruits, and traditional souvenirs. In the afternoon, board the Vande Bharat Express from Srinagar to Jammu. Upon reaching Jammu, transfer to the railway station and board the night train towards Howrah. Overnight stay in train.

Day 8ᵗʰ: Full Day Train Journey

Spend the day onboard the train while travelling back with unforgettable memories of Kashmir’s valleys, lakes, and mountains. Overnight stay in train.

Day 9ᵗʰ: Arrival at Kolkata

Arrive at Kolkata Railway Station, marking the end of a memorable Kashmir journey filled with scenic beauty, culture, and mountain experiences.
