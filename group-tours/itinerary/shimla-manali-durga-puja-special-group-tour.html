Tour Itinerary

Day 1ˢᵗ: Journey Begins from Howrah Railway Station

Meet and Board Netaji Express from Howrah Railway Station at 9:55 PM, marking the beginning of your exciting Himachal journey. Overnight stay in train.

Day 2ⁿᵈ: Full Day Train Journey

Enjoy the train journey throughout the day while travelling towards Chandigarh. Overnight stay in train.

Day 3ʳᵈ: Arrival at Chandigarh | Transfer to Shimla & Evening Sightseeing

Arrive at Chandigarh Railway Station and proceed towards Shimla. Take a evening walk, explore Kali Bari Temple, Mall Road, Christ Church, Gaiety Theatre, The Ridge, Scandal Point, and Lakkar Bazaar while enjoying the pleasant hill station atmosphere. Overnight stay in Shimla.

Day 4ᵗʰ: Shimla Sightseeing & Kufri Excursion

After breakfast, visit Jakhu Temple, Kufri Zoo, and Green Valley. Enjoy scenic mountain views before heading to Kufri for an overnight stay. Overnight stay in Kufri.

Day 5ᵗʰ: Kufri to Kasol | Scenic Himachal Drive

Proceed towards Kasol while enjoying beautiful stops including Sundernagar Lake, Pandoh Dam, Parvati River, Parvati Valley, Kasol Local Market, and Banglamukhi Ropeway area. Experience the serene beauty of Himachal landscapes throughout the journey. Overnight stay in Kasol.

Day 6ᵗʰ: Kasol to Manali via Kullu Valley

Transfer towards Manali while covering attractions including Manikaran Sahib, Kasol, Parvati Valley, Parvati River, Vaishno Devi Temple, Shawl Factory, River Rafting Point, Hot Air Balloon Point, Paragliding Point, and Kullu Valley. Later proceed to Manali. Overnight stay in Manali.

Day 7ᵗʰ: Solang Valley, Atal Tunnel & Adventure Excursion

Explore popular attractions around Manali including Nehru Kund, Palchan Bridge, Solang Valley, Dhundi, and Atal Tunnel. Visit to Sissu and Rohtang Pass, subject to weather and permit availability. Overnight stay in Manali.

Day 8ᵗʰ: Manali Local Sightseeing | Return to Chandigarh & Board Train

Visit Old Manali, Hadimba Devi Temple, Van Vihar, Club House, Tibetan Monastery, and Mall Road. Later, begin the return journey to Chandigarh Railway Station and board Netaji Express at 11:55 PM for Kolkata. Overnight stay in train.

Day 9ᵗʰ: Full Day Train Journey

Spend the day onboard the train while travelling back, carrying unforgettable memories of Himachal Pradesh. Overnight stay in train.

Day 10ᵗʰ: Arrival at Kolkata

Arrive at Kolkata in the morning, marking the end of a memorable Himachal tour filled with mountains, valleys, adventure, and scenic experiences.
