Tour Itinerary

9 Nights / 10 Days • Howrah to Howrah

DAY 1

Journey Begins from Howrah Railway Station

Meet our tour representative and board the train from Howrah Railway Station. Get comfortable in your seats as your exciting journey towards Himachal Pradesh begins. Overnight stay onboard the train.

DAY 2

Full Day Train Journey

Enjoy a full day onboard the train while travelling towards Ambala Cantt. Spend the day enjoying the journey and getting ready for your upcoming Himalayan adventure. Overnight stay onboard the train.

DAY 3

Ambala Cantt to Shimla

Arrive at Ambala Cantt. Begin your scenic drive towards Shimla, enjoying the gradual transition from the plains to the beautiful Himalayan foothills. On arrival, check in to your hotel and relax. Overnight stay in Shimla.

DAY 4

Shimla to Sarahan

After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Sarahan through picturesque mountain roads and the scenic landscapes of Himachal Pradesh. On arrival, check in to your accommodation and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Overnight stay in Sarahan.

DAY 5

Sarahan to Kalpa via Chitkul

After breakfast, set out for Kalpa via the beautiful village of Chitkul. Enjoy the spectacular landscapes of the Baspa Valley and the unique charm of this remote Himalayan settlement. Continue towards Kalpa and check in to your hotel. Overnight stay in Kalpa.

DAY 6

Experience the Sacred Raulane Festival in Kalpa

Immerse yourself in the rich cultural heritage of Kinnaur as you experience the fascinating Raulane Festival, a sacred and ancient ritualistic dance celebration of the region. Witness the traditional Raula and Raulane performers in their distinctive traditional attire, performing ceremonial dances associated with the local belief in the Saunis (sacred mountain spirits). Experience the unique customs, spiritual traditions, music and cultural spirit of this beautiful Himalayan village. Overnight stay in Kalpa.

DAY 7

Kalpa to Narkanda

After breakfast, check out and drive towards Narkanda, enjoying beautiful mountain views along the way. On arrival, check in to your hotel and relax amid the serene surroundings. Overnight stay in Narkanda.

DAY 8

Narkanda to Ambala Cantt

After breakfast, check out and begin your return drive towards Ambala Cantt. On arrival, proceed to the railway station to board your return train to Howrah. Overnight stay onboard the train.

DAY 9

Full Day Return Train Journey

Spend the day onboard the train while travelling back towards Howrah. Relax and enjoy the return journey, carrying unforgettable memories of the beautiful mountains, valleys and landscapes of Himachal Pradesh. Overnight stay onboard the train.

DAY 10

Arrival at Howrah Railway Station

Arrive at Howrah Railway Station, marking the end of your memorable 9 Nights / 10 Days Kinnaur–Kalpa tour. Return home with wonderful memories of the Himalayas and an unforgettable travel experience.
