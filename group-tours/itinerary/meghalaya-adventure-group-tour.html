Tour Itinerary

Day 01: Departure from Howrah – Overnight Train Journey

Your exciting Meghalaya Adventure begins at Howrah Railway Station. Board your scheduled overnight train to Guwahati and settle in for a comfortable journey towards the beautiful Northeast. Get ready to explore the breathtaking landscapes of Meghalaya, the enchanting "Abode of Clouds."

Overnight: Train

Day 02: Arrival at Guwahati – Scenic Drive to Shillong

Arrive at Guwahati Railway Station in the morning. And begin your scenic drive towards Shillong, the charming capital of Meghalaya. Enjoy sightseeing stops along the way as you gradually enter the beautiful hills and picturesque landscapes of the Khasi region. Upon arrival, check in to your hotel and relax.

In the evening, explore the lively streets of Shillong Police Bazar, enjoy local shopping, savour delicious food and experience the vibrant atmosphere of the hill town.

Evening Visit: Shillong Police Bazar
Overnight Stay: Shillong

Day 03: Shillong – Cherrapunji (Sohra)

After breakfast, check out from the Shillong hotel and proceed towards Cherrapunji, popularly known as Sohra, one of the most spectacular destinations in Meghalaya. Enjoy scenic sightseeing stops along the way, taking in the breathtaking valleys, rolling hills and magnificent natural landscapes.

By evening, arrive at Cherrapunji and check in to your hotel. Spend the rest of the evening at leisure, surrounded by the peaceful beauty of the Khasi Hills.

Route: Shillong – Cherrapunji
Overnight Stay: Cherrapunji

Day 04: Cherrapunji – Mawlynnong – Shnongpedong

After breakfast, check out from your Cherrapunji hotel and begin a beautiful journey towards Shnongpedong. En route, visit Mawlynnong Village, renowned for its cleanliness and charming natural surroundings. Explore the famous Riwai Single Living Root Bridge, a remarkable example of traditional Khasi bioengineering.

Continue your journey through the picturesque countryside, enjoying the region's beautiful waterfalls and scenic landscapes. By evening, reach Shnongpedong and check in to your riverside campsite. Relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings of nature.

Sightseeing:
Mawlynnong Village
Riwai Single Living Root Bridge
Scenic Waterfalls en route

Overnight Stay: Shnongpedong Campsite

Day 05: Shnongpedong – Adventure Activities at Umngot River

Wake up to the refreshing beauty of Shnongpedong and enjoy breakfast at the campsite. Get ready for an exciting day of adventure around the crystal-clear Umngot River, famous for its extraordinary transparent waters and stunning natural surroundings.

Experience a range of thrilling river activities, including kayaking, snorkelling, cliff jumping, boating, river trekking and many more exciting adventures. Spend the day enjoying the pristine river and the breathtaking beauty of the surrounding hills.

Return to the campsite and enjoy another peaceful night amidst nature.

Adventure Activities:
Kayaking
Snorkelling
Cliff Jumping
Boating
River Trekking
And many more activities

Overnight Stay: Shnongpedong Campsite

Day 06: Shnongpedong – Waterfalls & Canyons – Shillong

After breakfast, check out from your campsite and begin the scenic drive back towards Shillong. En route, explore the magnificent waterfalls and dramatic canyon landscapes that make Meghalaya a paradise for nature lovers and photography enthusiasts.

Enjoy the spectacular natural beauty of the region before continuing towards Shillong. By evening, arrive at the city and check in to your hotel. Relax and enjoy your final evening in the beautiful hill town.

Highlights:
Scenic Waterfalls
Beautiful Canyon Landscapes
Picturesque Mountain Roads

Overnight Stay: Shillong

Day 07: Shillong Sightseeing – Shopping – Departure for Guwahati

Enjoy your breakfast and make the most of your final morning in Shillong. Explore the remaining sightseeing attractions of the city and take some time for last-minute shopping. Pick up souvenirs, local products and memorable keepsakes from your Meghalaya adventure.

Later, check out from the hotel and proceed towards Guwahati Railway Station. Board your scheduled overnight train to Howrah and begin your return journey with wonderful memories of Meghalaya.

Highlights:
Shillong Sightseeing
Last-Minute Shopping
Transfer to Guwahati Railway Station
Board Overnight Train to Howrah

Overnight: Train

Day 08: Arrival at Howrah – Tour Ends

Arrive at Howrah Railway Station in the morning, bringing your wonderful Meghalaya Adventure to a memorable conclusion.

Return home with beautiful memories of Shillong, Cherrapunji, Mawlynnong, the crystal-clear Umngot River, thrilling adventure activities and the breathtaking landscapes of the Abode of Clouds.

Trip Ends: Howrah Railway Station
Until the next adventure, keep wandering and wondering!
