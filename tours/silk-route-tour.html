Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport / Malbazar – Sillery Gaon

Upon arrival at NJP Railway Station, Bagdogra Airport, or Malbazar, meet our representative and proceed towards the picturesque village of Sillery Gaon, nestled amidst the Himalayan foothills.

Day 02: Sillery Gaon – Zuluk

Wake up early to witness the magnificent sunrise over Mt. Kanchenjunga. After breakfast, proceed towards Zuluk, a historic settlement located on the famous Old Silk Route.

Day 03: Zuluk – Nathang Valley Excursion – Aritar

After an early breakfast, embark on a full-day sightseeing tour covering some of East Sikkim's most spectacular high-altitude attractions. After sightseeing, descend through the scenic mountain roads and proceed to Aritar.

Day 04: Aritar – NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport / Malbazar Drop

After breakfast, enjoy a relaxed morning amidst the serene surroundings of Aritar. Later, check out from the hotel and proceed towards NJP Railway Station, Bagdogra Airport, or Malbazar for your onward journey. Tour Ends with Cherished Memories of the Silk Route and Eastern Himalayas.
